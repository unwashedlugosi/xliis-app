XLIIs — Privacy

Effective July 29, 2026

What XLIIs Does

XLIIs is a free music player for streaming freely available Grateful Dead live recordings from the Internet Archive (archive.org). XLIIs has no user accounts, sign-in, advertising, or cross-app tracking.

Data Stored Only on Your Device

Your favorites, listening history, completed shows, track progress, and playback position are stored locally on your device. On iPhone, the app also copies the local favorites and current playback session to Apple UserDefaults so its CarPlay interface can use them. XLIIs does not upload this personal playback state to a cloud database.

Usage Information

XLIIs uses a random installation identifier and records product interactions such as app opens, show playback, feature use, liner-note use, playback duration, track counts, the selected collection, and show details associated with those events. XLIIs does not collect your name, email address, advertising identifier, or browser/device user-agent string.

The random identifier lets us count usage without an account. It is not used to follow you across other companies' apps or websites, serve ads, or build an advertising profile.

Current Listener Status

While audio is playing, XLIIs may send the random installation identifier, current show and track, collection, and update timestamps. This powers XLIIs' private operational dashboard and helps us understand whether playback is working. XLIIs attempts to remove the current-listener record when playback stops.

Purpose and Service Provider

We use this information only to measure product usage, diagnose reliability, and improve XLIIs. The data is processed and stored in Supabase, our database service provider. XLIIs does not sell or share this data for advertising or tracking.

Retention and Deletion

Current-listener status is temporary. Usage events are kept only while useful for the purposes described above and are deleted when no longer needed.

Network Requests

XLIIs requests audio and show metadata from the Internet Archive. Like other internet services, the Internet Archive may receive ordinary network information such as an IP address under its own privacy practices. Opening the XLIIs website, support page, or privacy policy also makes ordinary web requests to those sites.

Children's Privacy

XLIIs is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
